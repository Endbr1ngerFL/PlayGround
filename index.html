Field Manual

1) Loot & survive: kills give 🔩 scrap + XP. Scrap buys walls/turrets.
2) RPG levels: each level = +1 skill point. Open ⭐ SKILL (K) and boost DMG / RPM / HP / SPD / Turret.
3) Base: press B/BUILD at blue flag. Walls block, spikes bleed, turrets auto-fire, med heals, ammo box refills reserve.
4) Outbreak objectives: capture 3 purple anomalies (stand + hold USE 8s). Then reach green EXFIL and survive 60s for chopper = victory.
5) Persistent: world saves every 10s. Death = respawn at base, lose 15% scrap. Victory = prestige: keep levels, new outbreak harder.
